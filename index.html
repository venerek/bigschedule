Growth Planner — Where to Add Slots as You Scale

Computed live. Every hire is tested in all 10 schedules against the full coverage simulation, and the best is picked by, in order: 1 shortfall below floor · 2 days without a +1 call-out cushion · 3 depth of PTO dips under max PTO (3/weekday, 1/weekend day) · 4 avoiding new fixed-weekend (every-Sat / every-Sun) seats · 5 desirability · 6 spreading seats across schedules. Fixed-weekend seats are added only when they're operationally better; otherwise growth goes to the rotation pool.
